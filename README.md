# Two-edged Sword

A Bible study app for macOS and Windows, for reading Bibles, commentaries, dictionaries and books
beside your own notes. It comes with 60 modules: the KJV and some twenty more Bibles (among them the
Berean Standard Bible, Tyndale, the Geneva, the Douay-Rheims, the Vulgate, the Septuagint and the Peshitta, and Strong's and word-by-word editions), Strong's dictionaries, BDB and Abbott-Smith, the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, five commentaries (Matthew Henry, JFB, Clarke,
Barnes, Wesley), dictionaries from Easton's to the ISBE, a book of fifteen Bible maps, and four books: the works of Josephus,
Edersheim's Life and Times of Jesus the Messiah, Fox's Book of Martyrs and The Pilgrim's Progress.
It also reads modules in e-Sword X's formats: your own, and on a Mac e-Sword X's library if you have it.
It never changes them: your journal, highlights and plans are kept separately.

See it on the [Two-edged Sword website](https://two-edged-sword.davies.co.za/).

## Download

### Mac

For a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later.

1. Download [Two-edged-Sword.dmg](https://github.com/waynetd777/two-edged-sword/releases/latest/download/Two-edged-Sword.dmg) from the [latest release](https://github.com/waynetd777/two-edged-sword/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Two-edged Sword** onto **Applications**.
3. Open Two-edged Sword from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software, because it isn't notarised by Apple. Click **Done**.
4. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** beside Two-edged Sword, then **Open Anyway** again and enter your password.

It opens normally after that. The first time you use some features, macOS asks for permission: see [First run](docs/features.md#first-run).

### Windows

For Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

1. Download [Two-edged-Sword_x64-setup.exe](https://github.com/waynetd777/two-edged-sword/releases/latest/download/Two-edged-Sword_x64-setup.exe) from the [latest release](https://github.com/waynetd777/two-edged-sword/releases/latest).
2. Run it. It installs for you alone, with no administrator password.
3. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**: the app isn't code-signed.
4. Open Two-edged Sword from the Start menu.

A few things differ on Windows: see [On Windows](docs/features.md#on-windows).

The app checks for updates once a day and offers to install them. **Check for Updates…** checks now: at the foot of Settings, and in the app menu on a Mac or the notification-area menu on Windows.

## What it does

- **Read** a chapter beside every commentary on the verse, cross-references, dictionaries and
  your notes. Click any word for its Greek or Hebrew.
- **Highlight** in eight colours, each standing for a theme you name.
- **Books and devotionals** from your library: highlight, bookmark, note, listen to or ask about any paragraph, as you would a verse.
- **Listen** to anything read aloud, each word highlighted as it's spoken.
- **Differences from the KJV**: a mark wherever a translation differs in meaning from the KJV, with the reason.
- **Greek, Hebrew, Latin and Syriac** word by word, each word over its English and Strong's number.
- **Ask** questions about what you're reading, answered from your own library.
- **Compare** translations side by side, **Search** the whole library, and **Word Study** a Greek
  or Hebrew word.
- **Journal** entries linked to their verses, saved as Markdown files, so a notes app that works with Markdown, such as [Brainstead](https://brainstead.davies.co.za/), can open them too.
- **Quiet time** plans, with worship songs chosen for the day and a daily reminder. Songs can be chosen for any chapter too.
- **Build more modules** from free sources, such as the Targums, the Talmud and Beza's Greek New Testament.
- **KJV History**: the manuscripts and editions behind the KJV, and which of them you have.

## Quick start

Needs macOS 13 or later, the Xcode Command Line Tools, Rust and Node. The built-in modules
are downloaded and built on the first `make dev` or `make app` (`make core`). Ask also needs Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity or GitHub Copilot.

```sh
npm install
make dev                                  # the app with hot reload
cp signing.local.example signing.local    # then name your signing certificate in it
make install-app                          # build it and put it in /Applications
```

On Windows (10 or 11) it needs Rust, Node, Python and Visual Studio Build Tools with the C++
workload: `npx tauri build --debug --no-bundle` builds the app and `npx tauri build` the installer
(see [Development](docs/development.md#commands)).

If you have e-Sword X and no signing certificate, macOS asks again for access to its data after every build
([Signing and Full Disk Access](docs/development.md#signing-and-full-disk-access)).

Licensed Bibles are read where e-Sword keeps them and never copied into this repo.
