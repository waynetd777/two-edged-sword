## Differences from the KJV

<a href="images/index.md#translations-and-manuscripts"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="images/differences-dark.png"><img alt="Colossians 1:14 in the ASV, with the popup saying it omits “through his blood” and why" src="images/differences-light.png"></picture></a>

In Verse layout, a **≠** beside a verse marks where the translation differs in meaning from the
KJV, such as an omitted phrase or a changed name of God. It's faint for minor differences.

Click it to see both readings, what changed, and why (usually which manuscripts each follows).

Ask uses the same list, so you can ask "how does this chapter differ from the KJV?".

The app comes with lists for the NIV, ESV, ESV+, ASV, CEV, GNB, MKJV and ISV, each reviewed
across the whole Bible. They show when you have that translation.

## Greek and Hebrew

<a href="images/index.md#translations-and-manuscripts"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="images/interlinear-dark.png"><img alt="Luke 17 in Beza's Greek New Testament of 1598, word by word: each Greek word over its English and its Strong's number" src="images/interlinear-light.png"></picture></a>

Word-by-word Bibles show each original word over its English and its Strong's number.

- Hover a word for its grammar, or a number for its dictionary entry. Click a word to look it up.
- Greek and Hebrew Bibles without English of their own get the KJV's commonest rendering of each
  word. Settings › Appearance turns this off.
- Hebrew and Syriac read right to left.

### Other editions' readings

- **Greek NT TR+ and WH+** show another edition's reading in ⟨ ⟩. Hover it to see whose.
- **Greek NT INT+** marks in red the words that not every edition has, with a letter for each
  edition: τ Stephanus, σ Scrivener, β Byzantine, α Alexandrian, ν NA28.

## KJV History

<a href="images/index.md#translations-and-manuscripts"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="images/kjv-history-dark.png"><img alt="The KJV's family tree: manuscript traditions, printed editions, earlier English Bibles, and which are in the library" src="images/kjv-history-light.png"></picture></a>

**KJV History** (⌘8) is the KJV's family tree: the manuscripts, the printed editions the
translators used, and the English Bibles before it.

- The chips on each card show what you have: green is the text itself, amber the closest
  stand-in, blue a related module. Click one to read it.
- A yellow **A** means that Bible has the Apocrypha.
- Hover a card to light up what it came from and what came from it.
- **Ask** opens a panel for questions about the history.

## Targums and Talmud

Once you've built them from Sefaria ([Library](library.md#building-modules)):

- The **Targums** read and compare beside the KJV, verse for verse.
- The **Talmud** is a book per tractate, a chapter per page (daf), in English with the Aramaic.
- Ask finds the Talmud passages that cite the verses you're asking about.
