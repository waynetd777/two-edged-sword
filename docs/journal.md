## Write

<a href="images/index.md#journal"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="images/journal-dark.png"><img alt="A journal entry with its verse references as links" src="images/journal-light.png"></picture></a>

⌘N starts a dated entry. Each entry is linked to the verses it's about, and shows beside them in
Read.

The toolbar has headings, bold, italic, lists and quotes, and can:

- highlight, in the same eight colours as the readers
- insert a verse from the Bible, or link one
- add a tag

**Tags.** Type # to pick a tag from a list: your highlight themes first, then the tags you use
most. Arrow keys and Enter choose one.

**Verse references** you type become links. Hover one for the verse, click it to open it. Most
ways of writing a reference work, even common misspellings ("Isiah").

⌘F finds in the entry and ⌥⌘F replaces.

## Listen and focus

**Listen** (top right) reads the entry aloud, highlighting each word. Space or ⌘P pauses, and F7
and F9 go to the previous or next entry.

**Focus mode** (⌘.) hides everything but the entry. Esc leaves it.

## Ask about your journal

<a href="images/index.md#journal"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="images/journal-ask-dark.png"><img alt="Ask in the journal, about the open entry or the whole journal" src="images/journal-ask-light.png"></picture></a>

**Ask** (right of the toolbar) has two choices:

- **This entry**: about the entry and its verses. Select some text first to ask about just that,
  for example to explain it or suggest verses. **Insert into entry** adds the answer.
- **Whole journal**: about every entry the list shows, so a tag or filter narrows it. For
  example: "What themes keep coming back?"

An answer from Ask anywhere in the app can be added to the journal in one click.

## Spelling and grammar

macOS checks spelling (red underline) and grammar (blue underline). Click an underlined word for
suggestions. Words and phrases from the KJV, such as "maketh" and "thou art", aren't marked.

Grammar checking can be turned off in Settings › Journal.

## Where it's kept

Entries are Markdown files, one per month, in `~/Documents/Two-edged Sword/`. To keep them in
your Obsidian vault, choose a folder there in Settings › Journal.

Changes made in Obsidian show in the app within a few seconds.
