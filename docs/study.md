## Compare

- **Add** a translation from the menu.

- **Strong's numbers** shows them for the Bibles that have them.
- ← and → turn the page.

## Search

<a href="images/index.md#study"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="images/search-dark.png"><img alt="Searching the KJV for “tithe”, with the verse in context" src="images/search-light.png"></picture></a>

Searches a Bible, every commentary and dictionary, and your journal at once.

- Narrow it to part of the Bible, such as the Gospels or the Letters.
- Match the exact phrase, all or any of the words, or whole words only.
- Hyphens inside words don't matter: "Bethel" finds the KJV's "Beth-el", and "Beth-el" finds
  "Bethel".
- Search for a Strong's number such as `G509` to find every verse that uses that word, however
  it's translated.

Select a result to read it in context. Ask can summarise what the results say.

## Word Study

<a href="images/index.md#study"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="images/word-study-dark.png"><img alt="Word Study for G26, agapē" src="images/word-study-light.png"></picture></a>

Everything about one Greek or Hebrew word: its Strong's entry, where it's used, every verse in
context, related words, and the articles on it in your library.

Type a Strong's number, an English word ("love") or a transliteration ("agape").
