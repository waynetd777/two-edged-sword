# Two-edged Sword on your phone

Each section below folds open. The tabs along the bottom are Read, Plans, Search, Library and More.

## Read

- **Next and previous chapter**: swipe left or right.
- **Go to a passage**: tap the chapter name at the top. Pick a book and chapter, or type a reference like `rom 8 28`.
- **Verses or paragraphs**: a verse per line at first; the button at the top switches to paragraphs and back.
- **Hebrew and other right-to-left Bibles** read from right to left.
- **Interlinear Bibles** show each original word over its English and Strong's number.
- **A word**: tap it to see its Strong's number and meaning. Tap the speaker to hear it said, **Study this word** for its Word Study, or **Done** to close it.
- **Back and forward**: ‹ and › at the top left go back over every place you've been, as in a web browser.
- **Bible**: tap the Bible's name at the top to change it.
- **Highlight a verse**: tap the highlighter under the text, then a colour. It highlights the chosen verse. Tap the colour again, or ✕, to remove it.

## Study a verse

Tap a word in a verse to choose the verse, then tap Study (the graduation cap) in the switch at the start of the row under the text, or **Study this verse** on the word. Study takes the text's place; tap the book beside it to go back to the chapter. Swipe its heading left or right for the next or previous verse.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Commentary** | One commentary at a time. Tap its name to choose another; those with notes on the verse come first |
| **Cross-refs** | Related verses, from the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge or OpenBible.info. Tap **TSK** or **OpenBible** to switch, and a verse to go there |
| **Dictionary** | The verse's words that have a dictionary entry, to tap; or look up any word or name. The pill names the dictionary you're reading; tap it to choose another (those with the entry first, starred ones first among them). **‹ Back** returns to the last entry |
| **Maps** | Maps for the book. Tap a map to see it full screen, then tap a dot on a place to see the verses that name it |

On an iPad held sideways the study pane stays open beside the text.

## Compare

- Tap Compare in the switch at the start of the row under the text (the book goes back to the chapter) to see the verse in several Bibles, one under another.
- **Differences**: words that differ from the KJV (or the first Bible) are marked. Turn it off with the switch.
- **Edit** chooses the Bibles and their order. At least one stays ticked.
- Swipe, or tap ‹ ›, for the next verse.

## Listen

- Tap the speaker at the top to hear the chapter, or the book you're reading. The word being read is marked, and the page follows along.
- While it plays: tap the speed (0.75× to 2×), the sleep timer (15, 30 or 60 minutes, or the end of the chapter), or Stop.
<!-- ios -->
- It carries on with the screen locked, and the lock screen has pause and play.
- It pauses for a phone call, an alarm or unplugged headphones, and carries on after a call or alarm.
<!-- /ios -->
<!-- android -->
- Other sound pauses while it reads. It pauses for a phone call and carries on afterwards.
<!-- /android -->
- The sleep timer stops it even with the screen locked.
- **Voice**: choose it under More › Settings.
- In Compare, Listen reads the Bible you're reading. Compare follows the verse being read and marks the word in that Bible's card; ‹ › or a swipe moves the reading to another verse.

## Bookmarks

- Tap the bookmark under the text to bookmark the verse. Tap it again to remove it.
- Your bookmarks are under **Bookmarked** in Go to.

## Journal

- **Write on a verse**: tap the note button under the text. With no entries on the verse it opens a new one; otherwise it lists them, with **New entry**.
- **Verses with entries** have a note mark beside the verse number.
- **An entry**: a title, its verses and the text. Tap a verse to go there, ✕ to take it off, or type one in **Add a verse**. It saves as you type; **Done** closes it.
- **All entries**: More › Journal, newest first, with a search box.
- **Formatting**: the text is Markdown, as on the computer: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `- a list`.
- **Sync**: entries go to the journal on your computer. One changed in both places is kept twice, the phone's titled "(from iPhone)".

## Search

- Type words to find them in the Bible, or a reference to go straight there.
- Choose all the words, any of them, or the exact phrase, and which books to search in.
- Tap a result to open it.

## Word Study

Open it from **More › Word Study**, or **Study this word** on a tapped word. Type a Strong's number (`G26`), an English word (`love`), or a Greek or Hebrew word written in English letters (`agape`). It shows:

- the word's meaning from the lexicon, and the speaker to hear it said
- the verses that use it, by book
- what the commentaries and dictionaries say about it

## Library

- The tabs choose Bibles, Commentaries, Dictionaries or Books.
- **On this phone**: what you have. The bin removes a downloaded module.
- **To download**: more modules, free. Tap the arrow to download one.
- **ⓘ**: a module's About, with its cover. Tap the cover to see it full screen.
- **Read** opens a book or devotional. To go to another chapter, tap the chapter name at the top.

## Add your own modules

Modules from Two-edged Sword on your computer (files ending .bbli, .cmti, .dcti, .lexi, .refi or .devi) can be added. They're copied into the app.

- **Add from Files**: in the Library, tap **Add from Files** and pick one or more modules.
<!-- ios -->
- **AirDrop**: send the module to your iPhone or iPad. It's saved in the Files app, under Downloads. Then use Add from Files.
- **Finder**: with the iPhone or iPad plugged into your Mac, open it in Finder, click **Files**, and drag modules onto 2-edged Sword. They're added when you next open the app or its Library.
<!-- /ios -->
<!-- android -->
- **Quick Share or Files**: open a module, or share it to 2-edged Sword.
<!-- /android -->

A module you add shows as **Added** in the Library. One with the same file name as a module you already have replaces it.

## Sync with your computer

Bookmarks, highlights, the journal, reading plans, Recent and the chapter you're at in each book can be kept the same as Two-edged Sword on your computer.

1. On the computer, open Settings › Phones and tablets and click **Show a pairing code**.
2. Here, go to More › Settings › Sync, tap **Pair with your computer**, and enter the code.

After that it syncs by itself whenever the app is open and on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, with Two-edged Sword running there. **Sync now** syncs straight away. Your other phones and tablets catch up through the computer.

- The code works for 2 minutes, while Settings is open on the computer. Entered wrongly five times, it stops working: show a new one.
- Sync is encrypted: nothing sent over the Wi-Fi can be read by anyone else on it.
- Something is removed by sync only when it was deleted in the app, here or on the computer. An entry missing for any other reason comes back.
- **Pair again**: devices paired before sync was encrypted need pairing once more. The phone app and the computer's app must both be up to date.
- More › Settings › Sync shows the last sync: "Synced 2 min ago · 1 sent, 3 received", or why it failed.

When sync isn't right, a dot shows on **More**, and More says what's wrong, with **Sync now** or **Pair again**.

| Dot | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Amber | Syncs have failed several times and none has worked for over a day. A computer that's only off for a while doesn't count. |
| Red | It needs you: pair again, the computer couldn't save the journal, sync's records couldn't be read, or the app's storage is full. |

## Reading plans

- **Plans** (along the bottom) shows today's passages at the top. Tap one to read it, or **Listen** to hear them all. Tick each one as you read it.
- Under them it lists your plans, and starts a new one.

## Settings

Under More › Settings:

- **Theme**: System, Light or Dark.
- **Reading font** and **Reading text size**.
- **Words of Jesus in red**.
- **Voice** and **Speed** for Listen.
- **Daily reminder**: a time to be reminded to read.
- **Sync**: pairing with Two-edged Sword on your computer.
